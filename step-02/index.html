Headings outline the page

Use levels in order: one main h1, then h2 for sections, and so on.

Why headings matter

Screen readers and search engines skim your heading tree to understand structure. Avoid skipping levels for no reason.

Example

<h1>Recipe: bread</h1>
<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<p>Flour, water, salt, yeast.</p>
<h2>Steps</h2>
<p>Mix, rise, bake.</p>
